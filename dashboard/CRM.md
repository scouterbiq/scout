# Scout sections

Prospects contains companies whose SME status still needs verification, with review results and sources. Qualified Leads requires SME evidence; HRD Corp participation is optional and may be labelled as a bonus. SME training providers may qualify too. Clients contains records explicitly marked Client or deals marked Won. CRM contains active deal stages, notes and follow-ups.

All sections use the existing scout-crm-v1 browser-local storage. Back up and restore JSON to move edits. Private edits do not sync to GitHub; public company research data is stored in the repository.

The handoff target is 100 new ready SME leads monthly. A ready record needs SME evidence, a published business contact, location, industry and suggested training fit. Missing levy evidence never blocks it.
